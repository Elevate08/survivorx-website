Data Deletion

Publisher: Stasis State LLC · Last updated: November 6, 2026

Deleting Survivor-X Data

Survivor-X (Free) and Survivor-X have no Stasis State accounts or game servers. Optional Google Play Games sign-in uses your Google account; you do not need to delete that account to remove game data. To delete your game data:

  1. Uninstall the app, or
  2. Open Android Settings > Apps > Survivor-X (or Survivor-X (Free)) > Storage and tap Clear storage.

Either option removes local saves, progression, settings, and run-analytics files for that app. Repeat for both apps if installed: each keeps its own local copy, including imported progress. Existing backups or cloud saves can restore progress; manage them separately in your Android or Google backup settings.

Play Games is disabled in the current default configuration. If you used a Play Games-enabled build, delete the game's saved data, including its achievements, through Google Play Games or your Google Account. Deleting local storage does not delete that cloud copy. See Google's instructions for deleting Play Games data. Google controls retention of its service records.

Optional gameplay analytics

If you chose to share anonymous gameplay stats, Survivor-X (Free) and Survivor-X send gameplay records tied to a random analytics ID. To stop and delete them:

  1. In the game, open Settings and turn off Share anonymous gameplay stats. Nothing more is collected.
  2. Note the Analytics ID shown under that setting, and email it to privacy@stasisstate.com. We delete the gameplay records stored under that ID and reply within thirty (30) days.

Note the ID before you uninstall or clear storage: it is random and stored only on your device, so without it we cannot find your records. Records we are not asked to delete are deleted within 400 days of upload. Google Analytics keeps its event data for up to 14 months under the Google Privacy Policy.

Ads in Survivor-X (Free)

Survivor-X (Free) shows Google AdMob ads; Survivor-X shows none. We never receive the advertising ID or the data Google's ad SDK collects, so there is nothing for us to delete. To limit or remove it, reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Google > Ads); where the game shows Ad privacy choices in Settings, you can withdraw your ad consent there. Google handles that data under the Google Privacy Policy, and you can manage it through My Ad Center.

Correspondence we hold

Apart from the optional analytics above, we hold no Survivor-X game data on our servers. For email correspondence held by Stasis State LLC, write to privacy@stasisstate.com; we cannot erase local files or Google-hosted saves on your behalf. See the privacy policy for what is stored where.
