Deleting Survivor-X Data

Survivor-X and Survivor-X Premium have no Stasis State accounts or game servers. Optional Google Play Games sign-in uses your Google account; you do not need to delete that account to remove game data. To delete your game data:

  1. Uninstall the app, or
  2. Open Android Settings > Apps > Survivor-X > Storage and tap Clear storage.

Either option removes local saves, progression, settings, and run-analytics files for that edition. Repeat for both editions if installed: each keeps its own local copy, including imported progress. Existing backups or cloud saves can restore progress; manage them separately in your Android or Google backup settings.

Play Games is disabled in the current default configuration. If you used a Play Games-enabled build, delete the game's saved data through Google Play Games or your Google Account. Deleting local storage does not delete that cloud copy. See Google's instructions for deleting Play Games data. Google controls retention of its service records. For correspondence held by Stasis State LLC, email privacy@stasisstate.com; we cannot erase local files or Google-hosted saves on your behalf.
