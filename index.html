Core Systems

Forged for Tactical Depth

No bloated menus. No clunky virtual buttons. Every mechanic is engineered for instantaneous tactile responsiveness and emergent synergy.

Single-Thumb Tactical Combat

Auto-firing weaponry targets nearest threats with zero input lag, allowing you to focus entirely on precision movement, kite pathways, and hitbox evasion.

Weapon Merging: Tiers 1–4

Combine duplicate weapons in the post-wave shop to forge higher-tier armaments with amplified modifiers, increased projectile counts, and specialized behaviors.

14-Attribute Synergy Matrix

Balance Damage, Attack Speed, Critical Chance, Lifesteal, Projectile Pierce, Range, Kinetic Knockback, and Armor to construct indestructible builds.

High-Risk Cursed Items

Embrace double-edged sword relics that offer colossal offensive multipliers in exchange for crippled max health, erratic enemy speeds, or lethal arena hazards.

20-Wave Escalation & Final Boss

Survive 20 relentless rounds featuring evolving enemy formations, aggressive elite mobs, and an intense multi-phase Wave 20 encounter with the Astral Overlord.

Zero-GC Native Android Architecture

Preallocated memory pools and custom hardware-accelerated rendering eliminate runtime Garbage Collection pauses, ensuring rock-solid frame consistency.
