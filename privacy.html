Privacy Policy

Publisher: Stasis State LLC · Last updated: November 6, 2026

Survivor-X

This policy applies to Survivor-X (Free) and Survivor-X, the paid app, published by Stasis State LLC for Android. Both are playable offline and have no Stasis State accounts. The only data the apps send to us is the optional anonymous gameplay analytics described below, and only if you opt in. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send.

  • Local game data: Saves, progression, lifetime statistics, preferences, and cloud-sync state are kept in Android-protected app storage on your device. Run saves are encrypted at rest. To notice a device date that was changed, the game compares it with the device's boot count and uptime; these stay on your device and are not included in backups. A random installation identifier supports save synchronization; it is not an advertising ID.
  • Anonymous gameplay analytics (optional, off until you agree): The first time you open Survivor-X (Free) or Survivor-X, the game asks once whether to share anonymous gameplay statistics. Nothing is collected or sent until you say yes. If you decline, nothing is sent. You can change your choice at any time with the analytics toggle in Settings; turning it off stops collection immediately and disables Google Analytics collection (statistics already queued for upload while the device was offline may still be sent). What is collected: gameplay records for each run (hero, mode and Heat, waves reached, card drafts, shop choices, damage dealt and taken by source, boss results, revives, how the run ended), the app version and build, a random analytics ID generated on your device, the Firebase app instance ID, the device model, Android version and language, and an approximate location (such as city, region or country) that Google Analytics derives from your device's masked IP address. We do not collect your name, email, contact list, precise location, or the advertising ID for analytics; the apps request no location permission, and the analytics never read the advertising ID (Survivor-X (Free) holds the advertising ID permission only for the ads described below). Purpose: balancing the game and finding stability problems. Processor: Google LLC (Google Firebase, including Google Analytics and Cloud Firestore) processes this data on our behalf as a service provider. We do not sell this data or share it with advertisers or use it for advertising. Retention: we delete gameplay records within 400 days of upload; Google Analytics keeps event data for up to 14 months. Opt out and deletion: use the Settings toggle, then email privacy@stasisstate.com with the analytics ID shown in Settings and we will delete the gameplay records stored under that ID. Because the ID is random and we hold nothing else that identifies you, we can only find records if you give us that ID. Google Analytics data is managed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. The internal developer build, Survivor-X (Dev), only writes statistics to a local file and uploads nothing.
  • Advertising (Survivor-X (Free) only): Survivor-X (Free) shows ads from Google AdMob in two places: an optional rewarded video you can watch for the Extra Life revive, and a skippable full-screen ad that can appear when you return to the home screen after a finished run (never during a run). Survivor-X, the paid app, shows no ads and contains no advertising SDK. Consent: where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time with Ad privacy choices in Settings. What Google collects: when ads load, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google the device's IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), your interactions with the ads and the app (such as app launches, taps and video views), diagnostic information about the app and the SDK (such as launch time, hang rate and energy use), and device identifiers: the Android advertising ID, the app set ID and, where applicable, identifiers of accounts signed in on the device. Purpose: showing and measuring ads, personalized where your consent allows, analytics, and preventing fraud. Google acts as an independent controller of this data under the Google Privacy Policy; see how Google uses information from apps that use its services. We do not receive the advertising ID or the data the SDK collects, and the gameplay analytics above are never combined with it. Your controls: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Google > Ads); in consent regions you can also withdraw consent in the app. Ads are requested with a content rating of Teen or lower. Watching an ad is never required: you can decline the Extra Life, or switch to the paid app.
  • Google Play Games Services: Cloud-save and achievement support are implemented but disabled in the current default configuration. In a build configured for Play Games, Google collects SDK analytics and diagnostics for SDK stability and product improvements while the SDK is active. When you sign in, Google also processes your player identity (gamer ID, gamertag, avatar), saved progression, and lifetime statistics, and the achievements you unlock, together with the player XP and level and the game analytics Google derives from them. These support sign-in, saved-game synchronization, achievements, and diagnostics. The game only sends achievement unlocks to Play Games; it never reads them back, so they do not change your saved progress or unlock anything in the game. In-progress runs and local run-analytics files are not uploaded. Play Games communicates through Google Play services with encryption in transit; the game itself uses the internet only for the optional analytics and, in Survivor-X (Free), the ads described above. You can play offline without signing in. See the Google Privacy Policy and Play Games data disclosure. We do not operate a server receiving these saves or share them with advertisers. The game does not use Play Games leaderboards, friends, or events.
  • Android backup: If enabled in your device settings, Android may back up your profile, lifetime statistics, and settings through its backup service. Mid-run saves are excluded from cloud backup; on Android 12 and later they may transfer directly to a replacement device. Local run-analytics files and installation identifiers are excluded from backup and device transfer. Backup handling is subject to your device settings and service provider.
  • Progress import: The paid Survivor-X can copy progress from Survivor-X (Free) directly on the same device through a signature-protected Android provider. This does not use the internet.
  • Purchases: The paid Survivor-X is a separate app, rather than an in-app upgrade or subscription. No in-app billing SDK is integrated. The “Remove ads” link in Survivor-X (Free) only opens the paid app's listing in the Google Play app or your browser; the game sends nothing itself, and Google or your browser handles that page. Any Google Play purchase is handled by Google under its terms; we do not receive payment-card details.
  • Retention and deletion: Local data remains until removed from your device. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes local game data, but does not delete existing Google backups or Play Games saves. Backups or cloud sync can restore progress. If you used Play Games, delete the game's data through Play Games or your Google Account and manage Android backups separately. Google's retention rules apply to data it holds. Apart from optional analytics you opted in to (described above), we hold no Survivor-X game data on our servers. See our deletion instructions.
  • Support correspondence: We use your email address, message, and any attachments you provide to answer your request. We keep correspondence as needed to resolve the request and meet legal obligations. Do not send passwords, verification codes, or payment details. Contact privacy@stasisstate.com to request deletion of correspondence we hold.

Data Security

Survivor-X uses app storage protected by Android and encrypts its run saves. If you opt in to analytics, the game sends the records above to Google Firebase over HTTPS. Survivor-X (Free) also requests ads, and Google's consent form where required, from Google over HTTPS. Otherwise the game makes no network connections of its own. Play Games traffic, when enabled, travels through Google Play services over HTTPS. Backup handling is described above.

Children's Privacy

Survivor-X is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact privacy@stasisstate.com.

Website and Support

This static website is hosted by GitHub Pages. We add no analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or contact forms. GitHub processes visitor connection information, including IP addresses, to operate and secure hosting. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Email links open your email application; sending a message shares its contents with us and our email provider.

Your Privacy Requests

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold, or to withdraw consent. Contact privacy@stasisstate.com to exercise those rights; we respond within thirty (30) days without charge. Google-hosted data is managed through your Google account. We do not sell your personal information.
