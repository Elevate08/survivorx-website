Privacy Policy

Publisher: Stasis State LLC · Last updated: October 31, 2026

Survivor-X

This policy applies to Survivor-X (Free) and Survivor-X, the paid app, published by Stasis State LLC for Android. Both are playable offline and have no Stasis State accounts or game backend. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send.

  • Local game data: Saves, progression, lifetime statistics, preferences, and cloud-sync state are kept in Android-protected app storage on your device. Run saves are encrypted at rest. Random installation identifiers support local analytics and save synchronization; they are not advertising IDs.
  • Run analytics: The “Run analytics” setting records gameplay statistics, waves, drafts, and shop choices to a local file for balance analysis. It defaults to off in release builds and on in debug builds. The game does not upload this file or include it in Android backups. No remote analytics or crash-reporting SDK is integrated.
  • Advertising: Neither app currently integrates an advertising SDK, displays real network-served ads, or accesses the advertising ID. Survivor-X (Free)'s reward and advertising flows currently use a local placeholder. We will update this policy before introducing a live advertising service or additional data collection.
  • Google Play Games Services: Cloud-save and achievement support are implemented but disabled in the current default configuration. In a build configured for Play Games, Google collects SDK analytics and diagnostics for SDK stability and product improvements while the SDK is active. When you sign in, Google also processes your player identity (gamer ID, gamertag, avatar), saved progression, and lifetime statistics, and the achievements you unlock, together with the player XP and level and the game analytics Google derives from them. These support sign-in, saved-game synchronization, achievements, and diagnostics. The game only sends achievement unlocks to Play Games; it never reads them back, so they do not change your saved progress or unlock anything in the game. In-progress runs and local run-analytics files are not uploaded. Play Games communicates through Google Play services with encryption in transit; the game itself has no internet permission. You can play offline without signing in. See the Google Privacy Policy and Play Games data disclosure. We do not operate a server receiving these saves or share them with advertisers. The game does not use Play Games leaderboards, friends, or events.
  • Android backup: If enabled in your device settings, Android may back up your profile, lifetime statistics, and settings through its backup service. Mid-run saves are excluded from cloud backup; on Android 12 and later they may transfer directly to a replacement device. Run analytics and installation identifiers are excluded from backup and device transfer. Backup handling is subject to your device settings and service provider.
  • Progress import: The paid Survivor-X can copy progress from Survivor-X (Free) directly on the same device through a signature-protected Android provider. This does not use the internet.
  • Purchases: The paid Survivor-X is a separate app, rather than an in-app upgrade or subscription. No in-app billing SDK is integrated. The “Remove ads” link in Survivor-X (Free) only opens the paid app's listing in the Google Play app or your browser; the game sends nothing itself, and Google or your browser handles that page. Any Google Play purchase is handled by Google under its terms; we do not receive payment-card details.
  • Retention and deletion: Local data remains until removed from your device. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes local game data, but does not delete existing Google backups or Play Games saves. Backups or cloud sync can restore progress. If you used Play Games, delete the game's data through Play Games or your Google Account and manage Android backups separately. Google's retention rules apply to data it holds. We hold no Survivor-X game data on our servers. See our deletion instructions.
  • Support correspondence: We use your email address, message, and any attachments you provide to answer your request. We keep correspondence as needed to resolve the request and meet legal obligations. Do not send passwords, verification codes, or payment details. Contact privacy@stasisstate.com to request deletion of correspondence we hold.

Data Security

Survivor-X uses app storage protected by Android and encrypts its run saves. The game itself makes no network connections; Play Games traffic, when enabled, travels through Google Play services over HTTPS. Backup handling is described above.

Children's Privacy

Survivor-X is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact privacy@stasisstate.com.

Website and Support

This static website is hosted by GitHub Pages. We add no analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or contact forms. GitHub processes visitor connection information, including IP addresses, to operate and secure hosting. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Email links open your email application; sending a message shares its contents with us and our email provider.

Your Privacy Requests

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold, or to withdraw consent. Contact privacy@stasisstate.com to exercise those rights; we respond within thirty (30) days without charge. Google-hosted data is managed through your Google account. We do not sell your personal information.

Policy Updates

We will update this page and its revision date when our practices change, including before adding live ads or new data collection. Terms for the game are on the Terms of Service page.
